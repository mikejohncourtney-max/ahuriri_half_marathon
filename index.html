📍 Getting there, parking, toilets & bag drop

The event hub is at Te Karaka Reserve / Perfume Point on the Ahuriri waterfront.

Parking

  • In the car park between the event hub and the Hawke's Bay Sport Fishing Club
  • In the car parks on either side of East Pier on Hardinge Road
  • On-street parking around the Ahuriri business district

Please car-pool if you can, and allow extra time as Ahuriri will be busy.

Toilets

  • Portable toilets at the event hub
  • Public toilets near the Te Karaka / Perfume Point car park
  • Public toilets opposite The Sauna at the end of the Hawke's Bay Sport Fishing Club car park
  • Public toilets next to the Hardinge Road playground, on the other side of the Boardwalk Beach Bar & Bistro
  • Additional portable toilets will be available at the relay changeover point at the far end of the course.

Bag drop

A secure bag drop service is available at the start/finish area. Before you leave your bag, please make sure you get a drop bag label from the event team at the registration desk.

🗺️ The course, aid stations & on-course toilets

The course is a flat, out-and-back route on shared paths and footpaths. It runs from the Ahuriri waterfront out to a turnaround at Westshore, just past the airport gap at approx. 65 The Esplanade, and back.

  • Half Marathon: two loops
  • 10km: one loop
  • Relay: four legs of ~5km each

Aid stations

Aid stations with water, electrolyte drink, bananas and lollies will be at:

  • The Westshore beach reserve (playground near the Westshore Surf Life Saving Club), heading both out and back — approx. 3.8km and 6.7km into each lap
  • Near the start/finish line, shortly after starting lap two (for those doing the half marathon distance)

Aid stations will be on your left. Please don't cross the course to access an aid station.

On-course toilets

On the outbound leg, at approx.: 0.4km (opposite The Sauna), 1.2km (just before the Bluewater Hotel), 3.75km (Westshore Surf Life Saving Club), 4.5km (shortly after Domain Road), and at the relay changeover point at the far end of the course.

For detailed course information, see the course map and download the GPX file.

⚠️ Please read: sharing the path and staying safe
  • The paths stay open to the public. Expect walkers, cyclists, scooters and dogs. Please be courteous and give way where needed.
  • Keep left at all times. This is an out-and-back course, so faster runners will be coming towards you. Where possible, particularly in narrower sections of the course, please try to stay in single file.
  • Follow all marshal instructions. Take particular care at driveways and at the Hawke's Bay Sports Fishing Club, Napier Sailing Club and Westshore Surf Life Saving Club entrances, where vehicles and boat trailers may be crossing.
  • Headphone protocol. We know many of you like some music or a podcast while you run/walk. If you are wearing headphones on the day, please keep the volume low so you can hear marshals, cyclists and other runners.
  • No dogs or pushchairs on course. This is a decision we have made for the safety of all participants.
  • Stay on the marked course and away from the water's edge. The course is entirely on the pathways/footpaths, and there is no need to run on the road at any point. Running on the road may result in disqualification from the event.
🏃 Relay teams

Change-overs are at the Westshore turnaround, approximately opposite 65 The Esplanade (Leg 1 → 2 and Leg 3 → 4), and at the start/finish (Leg 2 → 3). Each change-over area will be shortly after rounding the turn cone at each end of the course.

  • Transport to and from Westshore is team-arranged. The event isn't providing transport to get you to/from your relay changeover points.
  • Park away from the course and walk to the change-over zone on the footpath, not along the running line. Please keep clear of runners.
  • Wait in the marked change-over area until your team-mate arrives.
  • There is no need to hand anything (relay baton etc.) to your next runner — simply tag them and they can start running.
  • Once you complete your relay leg, please make sure one of the marshals at the change-over area has recorded your bib number before you leave.
  • At the end of your leg, head back to the start/finish area to collect your medal and celebrate with the rest of your team. If you wish to run across the finish line together, you are welcome to do so. Earlier runners can join their Leg 4 runner as they round the final turn cone and enter the finish chute.
🩺 Health and safety
  • Wear your bib on the front where it's visible. Write any medical conditions and an emergency contact on the back.
  • First aid is provided by Medics On Scene at the finish, with access to the course.
  • If you need to stop, tell the nearest marshal. They'll let the Race Director know, and we'll arrange to get you back. Please never leave the course without telling a marshal, as we need to account for every runner.
  • A lead biker will be in front of the first runner on course.
  • A sweep will follow the last runner on course.
  • Plan for the weather. Spring weather can go either way, so bring sunscreen and a hat, and warm layers for before and after.
  • Keep an eye on children at the waterfront, as parts of the hub are next to the water.
🚨 In an emergency

If marshals need to stop the event, they'll halt runners and direct you to the nearest safe point. Please follow their instructions.

If you feel a long or strong earthquake (hard to stand up, or lasting more than a minute), remember: Long or Strong, Get Gone. Move on foot to high ground or inland straight away. Don't wait for an official warning.

🏅 After the race

Every finisher (including all relay team members) receives a custom medal, and there will be refreshments at the finish. Join us for prize-giving in the Ocean Suite at East Pier Hotel at around 11:30am. The East Pier bar and restaurant will be open to purchase food and drinks.

About the event

Run along the beautiful Ahuriri Estuary

The Ahuriri Half Marathon takes runners through some of Hawke's Bay's most stunning scenery — from the historic Ahuriri waterfront along the coast and back.

Whether you're chasing a personal best, tackling your first 10km, or teaming up with friends for the relay, this event is designed for runners of all abilities.

All finishers receive a medal, and post-race refreshments will be available at the finish line.

The route

Course overview

A flat, scenic out-and-back course starting and finishing at the Ahuriri Waterfront. The half marathon completes two loops; the 10km completes one. Relay change-over zones are at approximately 5km, 10km, and 15km.

There are three aid stations with water, electrolyte drink, bananas and lollies: two at the Westshore beach reserve (approx. 3.8km heading out and 6.7km heading back — on opposite sides of the path at the same spot), and one near the start/finish turnaround at approx. 10km for half marathon runners starting lap two. Aid stations are on your left — please don't cross the course to reach one.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What events are on offer? +
There are three events: the Half Marathon (21.1km, $40), the 10km run ($25), and a 4-person relay team for the half marathon distance at $20 per person ($80 per team). All events start and finish at the Ahuriri Waterfront in Napier.
How does the relay work? +
The relay is for teams of 4 people, each running approximately 5km. Change-over zones are located at around the 5km, 10km, and 15km marks of the course. Each team member receives a finisher medal.
When and where does the race start? +
The race starts at the Ahuriri Waterfront, Napier, on Sunday 11 October 2026. See the event timetable above for the full race day schedule, including registration, briefing, and start times.
Is there a minimum age to enter? +
Participants must be at least 16 years of age on race day to enter the half marathon. Participants must be at least 12 years of age on race day to enter the 10km. There is no age limit for relay team members.
Is there a time limit? +
Yes, there will be a 3 hour time limit for all distances.
Are walkers able to participate? +
Yes, walkers are very much welcome and encouraged to take part. Please note the 3 hour time limit for all distances.
Can I run with a dog or pushchair? +
While we want this event to be as inclusive as possible, from a health & safety perspective we will not be allowing participants to run/walk with a dog or pushchair.
Can I transfer or defer my entry? +
Transfer and deferral policies will be outlined at the time registrations open.
Is there parking nearby? +
Yes, there is parking available in the Ahuriri area. Detailed directions and parking information will be included in your pre-race briefing email.
Is transport provided to/from the relay changeover point at the far end of the course? +
No, participants will need to make their own arrangements to get to/from this relay changeover location. If you are taking a vehicle to this location, please park on the airport side of the road, to avoid any potential impact to runnners on course.
Can I download the GPX course file? +
Yes! You can download the GPX file from the course section above to load onto your GPS watch or device before race day.
